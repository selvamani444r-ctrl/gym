FAQs

Frequently Asked

What membership plans do you offer?

We offer three plans: Basic (₹999/month) for gym access and cardio, Pro (₹1999/month) for personal trainer + CrossFit + diet plan, and Elite VIP (₹3499/month) for the full luxury experience including nutrition coach, recovery therapy, and priority support.

Are the trainers certified?

Yes! All 35+ of our trainers hold nationally recognized certifications from bodies like ISSA, ACE, NASM, or equivalent. They regularly undergo continuing education to stay updated with the latest fitness science.

What are your gym timings?

ARIA FITNESS is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Staffed hours are Monday-Saturday: 5:00 AM – 11:00 PM, and Sunday: 7:00 AM – 9:00 PM. Pro and Elite members get round-the-clock access via smart access cards.

Do you provide diet and nutrition plans?

Yes! Pro members get a custom diet plan, while Elite VIP members have dedicated nutrition coaches. We create personalized macro-based meal plans tailored to your body composition, goals, dietary restrictions, and food preferences.

Can I book a free trial session?

Absolutely! We offer a FREE 1-day trial pass for all new visitors. Simply fill in the contact form, and our team will get in touch to schedule your complimentary session, which includes a full gym tour and a fitness assessment.

Is there a joining fee or lock-in contract?

There is a one-time registration fee of ₹299. All memberships are month-to-month with no long-term lock-in contracts. We also offer quarterly (10% off) and annual (20% off) pre-payment discounts.

Do you have facilities for women?

Yes! We have a dedicated women's-only workout zone with full equipment, separate changing rooms with steam bath access, and female certified trainers. Our classes like Yoga, Pilates, and Dance Fitness are very popular among women members.
